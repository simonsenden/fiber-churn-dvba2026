Keeping Fiber Customers

Proposal for a $20k targeted retention pilot.

We lose almost a third of our monthly revenue to churn. Almost three quarters of that loss comes from one group of customers.

LEVERS

Three levers aim at fiber customers behind most lost revenue.

One lever per driver. Hover a lever to see the evidence behind it; click a thumbnail to jump back to the driver.

The three shares overlap: one customer can be in several groups (e.g. a new fiber customer on a monthly plan without support). Never add them up. Revenue at stake = share of the $139k lost monthly revenue.

DECISION

Final ask: $20k to run our levers for 12 months.

One budget, two target groups: the three levers as presented, applied to customers we have and customers we win.

1 · THREE LEVERS, FOR BOTH GROUPS

  • Commit: 12-month contract
  • Onboard: welcome call, AutoPay default
  • Bundle: free support with the contract

2 · TWO CUSTOMER GROUPS

Current customersFiber risk group of ~1,160 active fiber customers on a month-to-month plan, in their first month, or without support
New customersAll new fiber customers, ~1,250 sign-ups per year, from their first month
